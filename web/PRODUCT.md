# Ember

## What it is

An LLM inference engine written from scratch in C++20, with no ML framework. It runs natively and in the browser through WebAssembly. Its author is Arham Doshi, a UT Austin CS and Linguistics student. Ember is one deep project built to show checkable proof of systems and ML skill.

## Who reads the site

Recruiters and engineers hiring for ML, software, and AI engineering roles. They give it under a minute at first. Engineers then look for something they can check. Nobody is a paying customer.

## What the site must prove

The engine is real and running in this tab, and every claim can be checked. Show the work happening instead of describing it.

## Truth rules

- No number appears unless a script in this repo measured it, or the visitor's own browser measures it live.
- Say plainly what Ember cannot do yet. Until Milestone 2, it does not load a model or generate text.
- Anything made up for a demo, such as scores or queries, is labeled as made up.
- No repo link until publish day, Nov 19, 2026. The repo and site stay private until then.
- No em or en dashes in written copy.

## Brand commitments

- Layout inherited from the author's Tink and MedData sites: serif headlines, hairline dividers instead of cards, a fixed side rail, and a live object in the hero.
- Palette chosen by the author: "Banked coals", a dark soot ground with one ember orange.
- Free tools and free tiers only. Fonts are self-hosted.
